## Summary

Multiframe does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything
the app does happens on your device.

## What the app accesses

**Camera.** The app requests camera access so it can show a viewfinder and
capture photographs. Camera frames are held in memory only for as long as it
takes to merge them into a finished photograph, and are then discarded. They
are not written anywhere else, and they are not sent off the device.

**Photo storage.** Photographs you capture are saved to your device's own photo
library, in a folder named after the app. The app writes only the files it
creates. It does not request permission to read your existing photo library and
cannot browse, open, or upload photographs taken with other apps.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not contain analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
- It does not share data with third parties, because it does not gather any.
- It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
- It does not request internet access. The app does not declare the `INTERNET`
  permission, so it is not technically capable of transmitting your photographs
  or any other data over a network.

## Your photographs

Photographs remain on your device, under your control. Delete them through your
device's photo app as you would any other picture. Uninstalling the app does not
delete photographs you have already saved.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone,
regardless of age.
